About Our Sanstha

Vimalabai Bahuuddeshiya Gramin Vikas Sanstha is a non-profit organization working for the development and well-being of rural and tribal communities.

Our Purpose

We focus on improving access to essential services and creating sustainable opportunities for vulnerable and underserved communities through community-based initiatives.
