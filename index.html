Vimalabai Bahuuddeshiya Gramin Vikas Sanstha is a non-profit organization working for the development and well-being of rural and tribal communities through health, education, livelihoods, agriculture, awareness and social welfare initiatives.

Our direction

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Our work is guided by community needs, inclusion and sustainable development.

🎯

Our Mission

To support rural and tribal communities by improving access to health, education, livelihood opportunities, awareness and essential social services.

🌿

Our Vision

A healthier, educated, empowered and self-reliant rural and tribal community where people can access opportunities and essential services with dignity.

🤝

Our Approach

We seek to work with communities, volunteers, institutions, donors and CSR partners to develop practical and sustainable initiatives.
