About Us

We work with rural, underserved and disadvantaged communities to improve living standards through opportunities, awareness and essential support systems.

Who We Are

Vimalabai Bahuuddeshiya Gramin Vikas Sanstha is a non-profit organization dedicated to creating sustainable social development. Our approach combines community participation with education, healthcare awareness, skill development, livelihood and empowerment initiatives.

We aim to build a self-reliant society driven by equality, dignity and sustainable progress.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To empower rural and disadvantaged communities by promoting education, healthcare awareness, skill development and equal opportunities for sustainable social transformation.

Mission: To develop innovative, community-oriented solutions that strengthen livelihood opportunities, social participation and long-term positive impact through education, empowerment and sustainable development.

Community Challenges & Our Response

Education Access

Challenge: Limited learning resources and opportunities.

Response: Educational support, learning materials, digital literacy and skill-based training.

Healthcare Access

Challenge: Gaps in healthcare awareness and preventive care.

Response: Health camps, awareness programs, nutrition support and preventive healthcare activities.

Livelihood Constraints

Challenge: Limited vocational skills and income opportunities.

Response: Vocational training, entrepreneurship support and capacity building.
